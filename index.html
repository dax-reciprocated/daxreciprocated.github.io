About DAX Reciprocated

I joined the Army in March of 2006 and served for eight years. Like many Veterans transitioning out, I found myself lost and frustrated trying to navigate the VA disability claims process on my own. While my claims themselves weren’t wrong, the way I submitted my documentation was—and that led to delays and setbacks.

Eventually, I hired an attorney, which extended the process but gave me the opportunity to learn the correct procedures, forms, and strategies needed for a successful claim. Eight years later, my claim was finally decided. While the attorneys received a significant portion of my back pay, I was ultimately rated at a level that made the journey worthwhile.

After that experience, I became a volunteer Veterans Service Officer with a local organization, where I was certified for multiple years and helped hundreds of local Veterans understand and prepare their VA disability claims.

I created this website to share that knowledge, help Veterans avoid the mistakes I made, and provide clear, practical education so Veterans can feel confident and prepared when navigating the VA claims process.

DAX Reciprocated focuses on education and strategy—not taking a cut of your backpay. Instead of a percentage-based model, you always know your cost up front through a transparent flat-fee structure.

DAX Reciprocated LLC provides educational information only. I am a former Veterans Service Officer but I am not acting as an accredited representative, attorney, or claims agent. I do not prepare, submit, or represent veterans in VA claims or appeals. I am not affiliated with the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs or any veterans service organization.
