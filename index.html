Why I built this

I joined the Army in March of 2006 and served for eight years. After dealing with my own long VA disability process, I learned how confusing the system can be and how costly mistakes in documentation and process can become.

Later, I became a volunteer Veterans Service Officer and helped hundreds of Veterans better understand and prepare their claims.
