The method

Facts first. Then understanding.

The four-step method is an educational way to make sense of the evidence. It isn't a VA-required filing sequence or a substitute for advice from an accredited representative.

01 IDENTIFY IT What condition is involved?
02 PROVE IT What documents its effects?
03 CONNECT IT What supports the relationship to service, when applicable?
04 EXPLAIN IT Can you understand what the evidence says?

No. This is general educational material. It isn't legal or medical advice, claim preparation, filing, representation, or a guarantee of a particular VA outcome. For your own claim, seek a VA-accredited representative.
