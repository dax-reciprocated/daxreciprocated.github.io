Why this exists

I joined the Army in March of 2006 and served for eight years. Like many Veterans, I went through my own long and frustrating VA disability process and learned firsthand how confusing the system can be.

Later, I became a volunteer Veterans Service Officer and helped hundreds of Veterans better understand their claims, decision letters, and documentation challenges.

DAX Reciprocated was created to provide educational clarity for Veterans who feel stuck, misled, or unsure about their VA decision.
